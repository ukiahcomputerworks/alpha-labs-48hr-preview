Welcome to Alpha Labs

Alpha Analytical Laboratories, Inc. is a premier California analytical laboratory network, specializing in regulatory compliance analyses of waters, sediments and solids since 1975. Our team is committed to providing consistently reliable data, personal service and analytical support to the California environmental sector.

Our clients range from local homeowners to the Environmental Protection Agency. We analyze potential contaminants in drinking water, bottled water, wastewater, recycled water, ground water and storm water, and characterize aqueous and solid matrices for hazardous-waste determination.

Six California locations encompass more than 25,000 square feet of processing space. Stringent Quality Assurance, Quality Control and Employee Ethics programs support reliable regulatory and non-regulatory monitoring.

Services

Drinking & Bottled Water

Certified testing for bacteriological parameters, inorganic analytes, trace-level metals, volatile organics, synthetics, pesticides and herbicides.

Wastewater & Storm Water

Laboratory support for NPDES dischargers, recycled-water generators, industrial users and monitored storm-water operations.

Pretreatment Programs

Regulatory-compliance support, 24-hour auto-sampler composite installations and onsite industrial and storm-water grab sampling.

Soil, Sediment & Solids

SW-846 methodology, ground-water monitoring, landfill leachate, site remediation and hazardous-waste characterization.
